✨ New Project: Deconstructing Reliance Stock Trends using Python! 📊

Without a fancy college degree, my focus has been entirely on building real-world skills. Recently, I dived deep into a dataset tracking 1,029 trading days of Reliance Industries to extract actionable stock market insights.

Here is what I built and discovered using Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib:

🔹 Trend Analysis: Used 20-day Moving Averages and Medians to smooth out daily market noise and identify clear stock price momentum.
🔹 Volatility & Risk: Tracked daily high-low spreads to isolate the exact days when the market faced extreme panic or excitement.
🔹 Seasonality: Grouped and aggregated data by months and years to visualize macro trends—including a detailed month-on-month trend mapping for the volatile year of 2020.
🔹 Volume Spikes: Filtered out the top 5 highest-volume days to understand when major institutional trading triggered liquidity surges.

This project taught me how to clean time-series data, handle datetime complexities, and turn raw numbers into clean, visual stories.
